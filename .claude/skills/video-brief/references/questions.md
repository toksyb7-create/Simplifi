# Question bank

Batch these into `AskUserQuestion` calls of up to 4 questions each. Every
question needs 2–4 concrete options; the user can always type "Other". Skip
anything already answered by the request or the repo. Rewrite the options to
fit the subject — these are starting points, not a script.

## Detailed

**Round 1 — Purpose & format**
- What is the video for? (Product launch · Feature announcement · Explainer/tutorial · Social teaser)
- Where will it play? (YouTube/landscape 16:9 · Instagram/TikTok 9:16 · LinkedIn/X square 1:1 · Website hero loop)
- How long? (15 s · 30 s · 60 s · 90 s+)
- Who's watching? (Developers · Buyers/decision-makers · Existing users · General public)

**Round 2 — Story**
- The one thing the viewer must remember? (offer 3 drafted one-liners based on what you know)
- Story shape? (Problem → turn → payoff · Show it working, no setup · Before/after · One continuous demo)
- Call to action at the end? (URL · Command to run · Waitlist/sign-up · None, brand only)
- Voiceover or captions? (No VO, on-screen text only · VO + captions · Captions only for a talking track · Music only)

**Round 3 — Brand identity**
- Existing brand guidelines? (Yes, in the repo/I'll share · Partial: logo + colours only · None, define one)
- Logo usage? (Logo at end only · Logo watermark throughout · Wordmark in the title · No logo)
- Tone of voice? (Plain & direct · Playful · Technical & precise · Premium & restrained)

**Round 4 — Colour & type** (use `preview` to show swatches / type pairings)
- Palette direction? (3 drafted palettes with hex values, recommended first)
- Light or dark base? (Light · Dark · Product UI decides)
- Typeface direction? (Geometric sans · Grotesk/neo-grotesk · Serif headlines + sans body · Mono-led technical)

**Round 5 — Look & motion**
- Visual style? (Real product UI, cropped big · Flat illustration · Typographic/kinetic text · Photo/video footage)
- Motion feel? (Snappy cuts on the beat · Calm and slow · Playful with some bounce · Precise, mechanical)
- Reference videos or sites you like? (free text via Other; offer "None")
- Anything you never want to see? (offer 3 likely items from the slop list + "Nothing specific")

**Round 6 — Sound & assets**
- Music? (I have a track — give path · Choose one for me (describe) · No music)
- Music tempo/mood? (Driving ~120 BPM · Mid ~95 BPM · Ambient/no beat · Match the track I give)
- Sound effects? (None · Subtle UI clicks only · Accents on key moments)
- Assets you'll provide? (Screenshots/recordings · Logo files · Fonts · Nothing, generate it)

## General direction

**Round 1**
- Overall style/mood? (3 drafted directions in one line each, e.g. "Warm editorial, serif, paper tones" · "Dark technical, mono accents, crisp cuts" · "Bright product-led, big UI crops")
- Platform and length? (16:9 · 30 s · 9:16 · 15 s · 16:9 · 60 s · 1:1 · 30 s)
- Music? (I'll give a track · You pick · No music)
- Anything particular you're expecting? (Must-have shot/line · Brand rule to follow · Nothing — surprise me)

**Round 2 (only if round 1 raised something specific)**
- Follow-ups on whatever they named in "anything particular".

## Your call

- What is the video about? (only if not already said)
- Platform/length — only if it can't be inferred; otherwise assume 16:9, 30 s and record it under Assumptions.
